# Glossary (English)

Common PsychedeliCare terms for translators and reviewers. Reviewers can update preferred terms as language teams make final decisions.

| English source term | Preferred term | Avoid / ambiguity | Usage note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PsychedeliCare | PsychedeliCare | translating the brand name | Keep the brand unchanged. |
| psychedelic care | psychedelic care | recreational-only framing | Broad care, support, education, integration, and policy context. |
| psychedelic-assisted therapy / PAT | psychedelic-assisted therapy (PAT) | psychedelic therapy when supervision is unclear | Use for therapy delivered with professional preparation, session support, and integration. |
| European Citizens’ Initiative / ECI | European Citizens’ Initiative (ECI) | petition | Formal EU democratic instrument. |
| mental health care | mental health care | mental illness treatment only | Includes prevention, care, wellbeing, and support. |
| substance / medicine | psychedelic substance; medicine only in clinical context | drug as a stigmatizing default | Use substance neutrally; use medicine when discussing therapeutic/clinical context. |
| set and setting | set and setting | environment only | Set is mindset; setting is physical/social context. |
| integration | integration | assimilation | Processing and applying insights after an experience. |
| harm reduction | harm reduction | risk elimination | Practical risk reduction, not a claim of no risk. |
| decriminalisation / legalisation | decriminalisation / legalisation | using them interchangeably | Decriminalisation reduces penalties; legalisation creates lawful regulated access. |
| compassionate use | compassionate use | charity access | Exceptional medical access for serious unmet need. |
| treatment-resistant depression | treatment-resistant depression | incurable depression | Depression not responding adequately to standard treatments. |
| lived experience | lived experience | anecdote | First-person knowledge that informs care and policy. |
| peer support | peer support | therapy | Mutual support from people with relevant lived experience. |
| wellbeing | wellbeing | wellness marketing | Holistic mental, physical, social, and ecological health. |
| stigma | stigma | shame only | Social and structural discrediting around people/substances/conditions. |
| nEUsletter | nEUsletter | newsletter | Preserve campaign spelling. |
