## BOOKS & DOCUMENTARIES

## Books

Here are some books we recommend if you are new to the subject of psychedelics as well as for more in-depth information on therapy, brain research and history of medicinal plant use throughout human history.

### New to these substances?

### Want to explore deeper?

#### [How to change your mind](https://michaelpollan.com/books/how-to-change-your-mind/)

Michael Pollan

#### [DMT The Spirit Molecule](https://www.rickstrassman.com/publications/the-spirit-molecule/)

Rick Strassman

#### [Psychedelic Medicine: The Healing Powers of LSD, MDMA, Psilocybin, and Ayahuasca](https://maps.org/product/psychedelic-medicine-the-healing-powers-of-lsd-mdma-psilocybin-and-ayahuasca/)

Richard Louis Miller

#### [Food of the gods](https://books.google.fr/books/about/Food_of_the_Gods.html?id=nmOTEAAAQBAJ&redir_esc=y)

Terence McKenna

#### [LSD, my problem child](https://books.google.fr/books/about/LSD.html?id=glPNoC2N-IgC&redir_esc=y)

Albert Hoffmann

#### [The Cosmic Serpent](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/349746/the-cosmic-serpent-by-jeremy-narby/)

Jeremy Narby

#### [The psychedelic explorer’s guide](https://www.psychedelicexplorersguide.com/)

James Fadiman

#### [The Doors of Perception](https://books.google.fr/books/about/The_Doors_Of_Perception.html?id=h5mMb-Jbxq4C&redir_esc=y)

Aldous Huxley
