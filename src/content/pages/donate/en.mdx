## DONATE TO THE INITIATIVE

## Your support matters!

Support our initiative through your charitable donation and help us raise the awareness of the benefits of Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy, address the mental health crisis in Europe and drive a democratic citizens’ movement.

Your donation can help us mainly in two ways: to print out communication assets such as flyers and other materials to increase awareness about the initiative and to organise supporting events and activities.

For us, donations via SEPA direct debit or SEPA bank transfer are most beneficial because they bypass commercial payment processors and their fees.

Your donation is handled by PsychedeliCare e.V., a non-profit association registered in Potsdam, Germany. PsychedeliCare e.V. is recognized by the tax authorities as a charitable association. Therefore donations are tax-deductible in Germany. Whether they are also tax-deductible in other EU countries depends on local regulations, because currently there is no EU-wide unified regulation.

**If you are really passionate about this issue, you can also [become a member of PsychedeliCare e.V.](/join-the-psychedelicare-association)**
