On World Mental Health Day and as part of the signature collection for the EU Citizens Initiative called [PsychedeliCare](/),The acclaimed movie Crazywise will be screened by [Station Zuid-west](https://stationzuidwest.nl/activiteiten/docu-crazywise-with-panel-talk/) at Stephensonstraat 7, 2014 KB Haarlem, Nederland

After the screening we will have a Q and A panel consisting of  
Jose Hoekstra, Charlotte Apeldoorn, Joya van Melle, Karina Bertolloto and Jessika Lagarde
