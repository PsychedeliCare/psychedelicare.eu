Join us for an inspiring gathering at the intersection of science, culture, and consciousness. This special event, in partnership with PsychedeliCare.eu, will celebrate the upcoming psilocybin scientific trials in the Czech Republic, set to begin next year. Together, we’ll explore the latest in psychedelic research, share cultural perspectives, and support the movement to expand opportunities for scientific study. Be part of a vibrant community dedicated to learning, dialogue, and positive change. **[Get your tickets here](https://psychonauticon2025.my.canva.site/page-5)**
