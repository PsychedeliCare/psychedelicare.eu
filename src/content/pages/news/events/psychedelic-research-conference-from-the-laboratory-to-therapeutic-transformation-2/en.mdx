**📍 Friday, October 24, 2025, from 9am to 1.30pm – Aula Magna, Faculty of Pharmacy and Food Sciences, Campus Diagonal, University of Barcelona**

This conference brings together leading researchers and clinicians to explore the most recent advances in psychedelic science — from preclinical discoveries to therapeutic applications.

Coordinated by Dr. Elisabet Domínguez-Clavé and Dr. Raúl López-Arnau

**In order to register, please fill out the form at the bottom of this page. Admission is free.**

**PROGRAMM**

**09:15 – 09:30** **Opening Remarks – Dr. Jordi Camarasa, Dean of the Faculty of Pharmacy**

**09:30 – 10:30** **Preclinical Research on Psychedelics**

Advances in Preclinical Psychedelic Research – Clara Riera, PhD Student, University of Barcelona

Exploring New Psychedelic Molecules: The Therapeutic Potential of Phenethylamines and DMT Derivatives in Depression – Dr. Núria Nadal-Gratacós, Lecturer, University of Barcelona

**10:30 – 11:00** **Clinical Research on Psychedelics (I)**

5-MeO-DMT and Psychedelic-Assisted Therapy in Treatment-Resistant Major Depression – Dr. Rosa Maria Dueñas, Psychiatrist & Researcher, ANIMA Group, Parc Sanitari Sant Joan de Déu

**11:00 – 11:30** **Coffee Break**

**11:30 – 12:30** **Clinical Research on Psychedelics (II)**

Ibogaine for the Treatment of Substance Use Disorders: From Mechanisms to Clinical Efficacy – Dr. Genís Oña, Psychologist & Researcher, Sant Joan de Déu Research Institute (IRSJD), Universitat Rovira i Virgili

Promises and Challenges of Psilocybin in the Treatment of Depression – Dr. Santiago Madero, Psychiatrist & Researcher, Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, IDIBAPS

**12:30 – 13:15** **Roundtable**  
**Cross-questions between Preclinical and Clinical Researchers**

**13:15 – 13:30** **Closing Session**  
Presentation of the European Citizens' Initiative PsychedeliCare & Closing Remarks
