On March 20th, **Psychedelic Society Maastricht** will host the **Museum of Consciousness** at Maastricht University in The Netherlands. Created and facilitated by esteemed researcher and innovator [Carl Hayden Smith](https://www.projectimmersed.org/hosts-and-contributors/carl-hayden-smith), the Museum of Consciousness is a unique, immersive exhibition designed to induce expansive states of consciousness through curated audio experiences. This event offers a unique opportunity to explore consciousness through immersive sound experiences.

Half of all the revenue from ticket sales will be donated to the **PsychedeliCare Initiative**. Donations will specifically help fund promotional materials, advertisements, and community engagement efforts, which are essential for driving our important initiative forward. This event was made possible through a generous donation from PsyDAO, a decentralized organization dedicated to advancing psychedelic science and culture through blockchain technology.
