Alexis Arragon (French Team Lead) was invited to present a lecture at the 2025 **[Congress of Fédération Addiction](https://congres.federationaddiction.fr/)** that took places on the 22-23 of May 2025 in Angers, France. The lecture entitled [“**Autonomous and prescribed uses of drugs**” is available for viewing (in French)](https://cloud.psychedelicare.eu/s/8TfkYZdQF8TNrcA). Alexis spoke about the context of psychedelics in Europe and especially in France, and introduced PsychedeliCare as a step towards recognition of psychedelics as non-addictive, mostly safe substances.
