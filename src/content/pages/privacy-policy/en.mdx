## PRIVACY POLICY

### Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how psychedelicare.eu collects, uses, and protects the personal data you provide to us through our contact form. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### How We Use Your Personal Data

We use the personal data you provide through the contact form for the purpose of contacting you and of responding to your inquiry or question. We do not use it for commercial purposes or other marketing activities without your explicit consent.

### Consent

By using our contact form and providing your personal data, you consent to us using it for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.

### Sharing Personal Data

We do not share your personal data with third parties unless it is necessary to respond to your request or if we are legally obligated to do so.

### Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized use, loss, access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, please be aware that the security of information transmitted over the Internet cannot be guaranteed 100%.

### Your Rights

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@psychedelicare.eu. We will respond to your request within the timeframes required by law.

### Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the date of the last update will be indicated at the top of this page.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at the following email address: info@psychedelicare.eu
