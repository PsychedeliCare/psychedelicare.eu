Join **PsychedeliCare e.V.**, the non-profit organization behind the **European Citizens’ Initiative (ECI) PsychedeliCare.EU**! By becoming a member, you actively support our mission to promote **evidence-based drug policy reform, harm reduction, and equitable access to psychedelic therapies** in Europe.

### **Why Become a Member?**

-   **Shape the Future**: Help drive political change in the EU by supporting our advocacy work.
-   **Stay Informed**: Receive updates on our campaigns, events, and scientific developments.
-   **Network & Collaborate**: Connect with like-minded individuals and organizations in the field of drug policy and psychedelic research.
-   **Strengthen Our Voice**: The more members we have, the greater our influence in EU policymaking.

**Together, we can create a more rational, compassionate, and science-based approach to psychedelics in Europe!**

_Fill out the form below to join us today._

**In order to terminate your membership, please notify the board in writing ([info@psychedelicare.eu](mailto:info@psychedelicare.eu?subject=Resignation%20from%20PsychedeliCare%20e.V.)) at the latest one month be+fore end of the financial year. Please see the [statutes](/assets/files/statutes-psychedelicare.pdf) for full details (section "termination of membership")**
